Privacy Policy for nimode

Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how nimode ("we", "us", or "our"), developed by sieradni, handles your data when you use the nimode Discord Activity.

1. Information We Collect

When you access or use nimode through Discord, the Activity may request access to basic information provided by the Discord Embedded App SDK, including:

  • Basic Discord Profile Information: Discord User ID, Username, and Avatar URL.
  • Discord Channel & Context Info: Guild (Server) ID and Voice Channel ID to enable local activity functionality.

2. How Your Data Is Stored & Processed

nimode does not operate a backend database or remote server.

  • All application data, preferences, and local state are stored entirely on your local device using standard browser storage (such as localStorage).
  • We do not transmit, collect, or store your personal data on any external servers.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking services, or ad networks are integrated into the Activity.
  • Multiplayer Spectating (P2P): When you join a Discord Activity session, your game state (board, active piece, queue, hold, annotations, and statistics) may be transmitted directly to other participants via peer-to-peer WebRTC connections if your instance is set to Public. A public STUN server (stun.l.google.com:19302) is used solely to establish these connections; no game data passes through it.
  • You can disable spectating at any time using the Private Instance toggle, which stops all outbound game state transmissions.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your data is stored locally in your browser/Discord client session, you retain complete control over your data:

  • You can clear your data at any time by clearing your browser cache/local storage or closing the Activity session.
  • Since we do not store any personal data on external servers, there is no server data to request deletion for.

4. Third-Party Links & Services

nimode operates within the Discord client. Your interaction with Discord itself is governed by Discord's Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

nimode is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirement (13 years of age, or higher depending on local legal requirements). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
